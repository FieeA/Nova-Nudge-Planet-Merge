Nova Nudge – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Nova Nudge (package name com.blackcomet.orbitmerge, the "Game") handles information. The Game is published by Black Comet Games ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at blackcometgames.dev@gmail.com.

1. Summary

You can play the Game without creating an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The Game is free and supported by advertising. To show ads, measure how the Game is used and fix crashes, the Game includes software from Google that collects certain device and usage data, as described below.

2. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress – high scores, coins, daily challenge streak, sound and music settings, and whether you have bought "Remove Ads" – is saved locally on your device. We do not upload this data to our own servers. It is deleted when you clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game.

3. Information collected through third-party services

The Game uses the following services provided by Google. They collect data automatically and process it under Google's own privacy policy.

ServicePurposeData that may be collected
Google AdMob Showing banner, interstitial and rewarded ads; ad measurement and fraud prevention Advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device model and operating system, language, ad interactions, consent choices
Google Analytics for Firebase Understanding how the Game is played (for example games started, ads watched, daily challenges completed) so we can improve it App instance ID, advertising ID, in-game events, device model and operating system, country/region, app version
Firebase Crashlytics Finding and fixing crashes Crash logs, device model and operating system, app version, an installation identifier
Google Play Billing Processing the optional "Remove Ads" purchase The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We only receive confirmation that the item was bought; we never see your card or payment details.

4. Advertising and your choices

Ads in the Game may be personalized, meaning they are based on your interests as inferred by Google and its advertising partners, or non-personalized.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: when you first open the Game we ask for your consent before personal data is used for advertising. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the privacy link on the Game's home screen.
  • All users: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Buying "Remove Ads" removes banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch one.

5. How we use information

Information is used only to operate the Game, show and measure advertising, understand overall usage, and diagnose technical problems. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not combine it with other data to identify you personally.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on your consent for personalized advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device for advertising purposes, and on our legitimate interests for basic analytics, crash reporting and keeping the Game secure and working.

7. Data retention

We do not keep personal data on servers of our own. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies; for example, Firebase Analytics user-level data is retained for up to 14 months and Crashlytics crash data for up to 90 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold data that identifies you directly, the most effective ways to exercise these rights are the controls described in section 4 and uninstalling the Game. You can also email us and we will help as far as we are able. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us.

10. International transfers

Google may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. Google applies its own safeguards to such transfers as described in its privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
